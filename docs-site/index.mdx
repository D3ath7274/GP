## Know which capability you are using

- **Site collection:** bounded site-v3 observations and shadow profiles, UNKNOWN ground truth, no site-model inference or automatic firewall writes.
- **Policy workbench:** read-only operator-described policy models, audit witnesses and hypothetical change review.
- **Controlled response tests:** a separately operated nftables reference adapter and independent security-campaign evidence.
- **Original lab reference:** Ryu, Snort, rate/DAI, RF and AE remain under **Reference**. Their models and enforcement assumptions are separate from the current site collector.
